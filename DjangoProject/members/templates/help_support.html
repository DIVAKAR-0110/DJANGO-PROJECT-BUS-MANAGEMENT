Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for a new bus pass?
Navigate to the registration section on the homepage, fill in your academic details, upload your student photo, and submit. Your college admin will review it within 24-48 hours.
What is the validity period of a pass?
Standard passes are valid for one full semester (approximately 6 months). You can check the exact expiry date on your digital pass in the dashboard.
Can I change my bus route mid-semester?
Route changes are subject to seat availability. Please contact your college transportation office to request a route adjustment.
What happens if I lose my digital pass?
No worries! You can always log in to your student dashboard and download a fresh copy of your QR ticket at any time.
